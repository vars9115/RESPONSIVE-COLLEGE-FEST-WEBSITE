About Our Fest

College Fest 2026 is an annual celebration organized by our college. It provides students with an opportunity to showcase their talents, creativity, technical skills and sports abilities.

The fest brings students together through competitions, cultural programs, workshops and entertainment activities.

Our Vision

To create a platform where students can learn, compete, communicate and build lasting memories.
